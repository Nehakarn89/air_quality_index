From analysis to action

How this kind of analysis could support operational planning

Public-health teams: monitor respiratory demand alongside pollution episodes and plan staffing for peak periods.
Pharmacies: use verified demand history to improve planning for masks and commonly used respiratory-care supplies.
City teams: compare area-level exposure indicators to help prioritise monitoring and communication.

These are planning questions, not medical advice or causal conclusions.
Data note: this is a demonstration interface built with synthetic, illustrative values to show how environmental and health datasets could be explored together. It does not establish that pollution caused changes in illness. A research version should use validated CPCB air-quality observations, clearly defined hospital/health datasets, consistent geography and dates, and appropriate privacy safeguards.
